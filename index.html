Privacy Policy

MRX QR Scanner
Last Updated: October 2026

MRX QR Scanner is developed by Sonu Lal. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information while you use the MRX QR Scanner application.

1. Information We Access

MRX QR Scanner may request access to your device camera so that you can scan QR codes and barcodes.

The application may also allow you to select images from your device gallery for QR code or barcode scanning.

2. Camera Permission

Camera access is used only for scanning QR codes and barcodes. MRX QR Scanner does not intentionally use the camera to collect personal information unrelated to scanning.

3. Gallery and Images

If you choose an image from your device gallery for scanning, the selected image is used to detect QR codes or barcodes. We do not intentionally upload your personal photos to our own servers.

4. Scan Results

QR codes and barcodes may contain information such as website URLs, text, contact information, or other data. Scan results are displayed to you so that you can decide what action to take.

5. Scan History

If the application provides scan history, scan results may be stored locally on your device to allow you to view previous scans.

This locally stored history is controlled by you and can be removed by clearing the application's data or using any available delete option in the application.

6. Internet and External Links

When you choose to open a website or other external link from a scan result, that website is operated by a third party. MRX QR Scanner is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

7. Third-Party Services

MRX QR Scanner may use third-party services or libraries required for application functionality, analytics, security, or advertising, if enabled in the application.

Such third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

8. Children's Privacy

MRX QR Scanner is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable privacy laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
